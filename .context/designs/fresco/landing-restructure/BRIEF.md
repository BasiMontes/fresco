# Design brief: Fresco / landing-restructure

Tool session: none (Mode C, screens generated by the AI through the loaded design set)

## Mission

Redesign the structure of the guest landing `/` so it stops reading as a template. Anti Web Slop scored `fresco-pre` at 48 % (53 % on `fresco-pro` before FRESCO-1131 removed section labels and "Tres pasos"). The score measures template signals, not taste. Ticket: FRESCO-1134, epic FRESCO-884.

## Method and skills loaded (provenance)

Mode C of `design-system/references/screen-design-mapping.md`. Loaded before the first line of HTML: `frontend-design`, `ui-ux-pro-max`, `impeccable`, `emil-design-eng`, `redesign-existing-projects`, `design-taste-frontend`. Where a generic rule of those skills conflicts with the frozen contract (Fraunces is banned by the taste skill, the cream palette is flagged as an AI default, Lucide is discouraged), `DESIGN.md` wins: Critical Rule 14, the fidelity reference is the live UI plus `DESIGN.md` tokens.

## Design read

Persuade surface (landing) for Spanish households that plan weekly meals. Calm editorial language, low motion, trust over spectacle. Dials: variance 6, motion 3, density 3.

## Frozen design contract (inlined from DESIGN.md v2)

- Colors: primary `#0F4E0E`, secondary `#DF8C26`, tertiary `#6F5F43`, background `#FAF3E3`, surface `#F1E3C6`, surface-raised `#FBF6EC`, text `#201E1D`, border text at 16 %, error `#B03D2B`, accent ramp 100 to 900 (green), accent-2 ramp 100 to 900 (amber). Amber as text uses `accent-2-700` `#8A5513`, never `secondary`.
- Typography: Fraunces on h1 and h2 only (weight 400 or 300, never bold), Figtree everywhere else. Body 15 px, small 13 px, minimum 12 px.
- Radius: buttons and tags full pill, card 20 px, image 16 px, input pill.
- Spacing page rhythm: 52.8 px between minor blocks, 70.4 px between major blocks, 105.6 px hero breathing room.
- Components: every card is `surface-raised` with a hairline border. One orange `button-action` per screen. Orange is never decoration.

## What changes (functional intent)

1. The visitor sees the product before reading about it: the brand video moves right after the hero.
2. "Un domingo sin agobios" becomes a vertical walkthrough with one real dish photo, instead of four equal cards.
3. The pain moments stop being three equal cards and become three rows of text.
4. "Aprende de lo que cocinas" (Pro) becomes a timeline, because the weeks are a real sequence.
5. The ImpactStats section is removed. Its three claims repeat the hero and the steps. The one claim not repeated elsewhere ("sin comidas ni cenas repetidas dentro de la misma semana") moves into step 2.
6. Pricing stops being two equal towers: the Pro plan is wider.
7. New order: hero, video, walkthrough, pain, Pro timeline, pricing, FAQ, final CTA, footer.

## Unchanged on purpose

Nav, hero copy and photos, pricing content, FAQ copy (11 questions), final CTA and waitlist form, footer. Every claim comes from the live copy; no new claims, no invented numbers.

## Hard constraints

- File name is the screen slug: `landing-restructure.html`.
- No token outside the frozen contract. UI copy in Spanish from Spain, no em dashes.
- Accessibility AA: 44 px targets, visible focus, reduced motion honoured, text never below 12 px.
- Real photos only (the catalog photos already used in `components/landing/hero.tsx`), no invented screenshots or logos.
