# Design brief: Fresco / first-visit-tour

Tool session: none (Mode C, screens generated by the AI through the loaded design set)

## Mission

Design 2 screens for FRESCO-1197 (epic: Pulido de flujos de usuario): a short first-visit guided tour for registered people who just finished the profile onboarding, and the row that lets them replay it from the profile Help section, inside the Configuración modal. We describe WHAT each screen must accomplish; the AI composes it inside the frozen contract below.

## Method and skills loaded (provenance)

Mode C of `design-system/references/screen-design-mapping.md`. Loaded before the first line of HTML: `frontend-design`, `ui-ux-pro-max`, `impeccable`, `emil-design-eng`. Where a generic rule of those skills conflicts with the frozen contract, `DESIGN.md` wins (Critical Rule 14: the fidelity reference is the live UI plus `DESIGN.md` tokens). `impeccable context` reported no PRODUCT.md; `init` was not run on purpose (out of scope of this ticket, `DESIGN.md` is the contract).

## Design read

Operate surface (inside the app, first run). Calm editorial language, low motion, trust over spectacle. The tour must feel like a short guided walk, not a tutorial wall: it points at the real screen, says one thing per stop, and gets out of the way. The idea comes from a reference screenshot of another platform; its look (voice, mascot, layout) is NOT copied.

## Decisions already taken by the owner (2026-11-12)

1. First time = right after finishing the profile onboarding, registered accounts only (guests excluded).
2. "Already seen" is remembered on the account (follows across devices).
3. No voice and no mascot.
4. The tour can be replayed from the Configuración modal of the profile Help section.

## Frozen design contract (inlined from DESIGN.md v2)

- Colors: primary `#0F4E0E`, secondary `#DF8C26`, tertiary `#6F5F43`, background `#FAF3E3`, surface `#F1E3C6`, surface-raised `#FBF6EC`, text `#201E1D`, border text at 16 %, accent ramp green 100 to 900, accent-2 amber ramp. Amber as text uses `accent-2-700` `#8A5513`.
- Typography: Young Serif on h1 and h2 only (weight 400), Figtree everywhere else (h3 22/600, label 14/600, body 15, small 13, caption 12 minimum).
- Radius: buttons and tags full pill, card 20, image 16, input pill.
- Components: every card is `surface-raised` with a hairline border; one orange `button-action` per screen at most, never decorative.
- Motion: calm, 150 to 250 ms, `ease-smooth-out`, no new overshoot, `prefers-reduced-motion` honoured.

## Screens requested

### 1. first-visit-tour: Recorrido de primera visita
- Route: any app route right after onboarding (first landing on `/menu`).
- Purpose: explain in a few stops how the app works and what to do.
- Stories: FRESCO-1197.
- The person must be able to: read one short stop at a time (title plus two or three sentences) while the zone it talks about stays visible on the real screen and the rest is dimmed; see where they are in the walk; go to the next stop; skip the whole walk; close it at any moment; finish it.
- Stops (few, from the ticket): generate and read the week's menu; what each slot of the day is and how to mark a dish as cooked or discarded; change a dish; the shopping list; what Pro adds (marks shape the person's own menus; in Free they are stored but not applied).
- States: first stop, middle stop, last stop (finish replaces next), keyboard focus on every control, reduced motion, mobile with the bottom tab bar as the highlighted zone.
- Viewport: desktop 1440 and mobile 390.

### 2. settings-tour-row: Volver a ver el recorrido
- Route: `/profile`, Ayuda section, Configuración modal.
- Purpose: let anyone replay the tour whenever they want.
- The person must be able to: find an action that starts the tour again from the Configuración modal, next to the other account items, and understand it will not change any data.
- Viewport: desktop and mobile (modal as it exists today).

## Hard constraints

- File names are the screen slugs: `first-visit-tour.html`, `settings-tour-row.html`.
- No token outside the frozen contract. UI copy in Spanish from Spain, no em dashes, no voseo.
- Accessibility AA: 44 px targets, visible focus, text never below 12 px, dialog semantics with labelled title, Escape closes, focus returns to the origin.
- No claim beyond the live copy: nothing about savings, numbers or features that the app does not state today.
- No kicker or eyebrow above headings, no numbered decoration beyond the real "n de N" progress.
