# Design brief: Fresco / email-typography

Tool session: none (Mode C, screens generated by the AI through the loaded design set)

## Mission

Give the five transactional emails the brand typography of the web, with the same hierarchy and tone, and a fallback that still looks like the brand when the email client ignores web fonts. Ticket: FRESCO-1198, epic FRESCO-869. It started from phone screenshots of the "Confirmación de tu suscripción a Fresco Pro" email.

## Method and skills loaded (provenance)

Mode C of `design-system/references/screen-design-mapping.md`. Loaded in this session before the first line of HTML: `frontend-design`, `ui-ux-pro-max`, `impeccable`, `emil-design-eng`. `DESIGN.md` wins over any generic rule (Critical Rule 14). An email is not an app screen: it is table layout with inline styles, so the mockup shows what each template would draw, not app components.

## Constraints

- Email clients ignore or limit web fonts (Gmail ignores them): the fallback stack has to look like the brand, not like Arial.
- The Auth templates are dashboard-managed and a Management API PATCH applies them to the three environments at once (`supabase/templates/README.md`).
- The waitlist email is plain on purpose (FRESCO-1008: no logo, no tracking); only its type changes.
- The header band keeps the real negative logo (`Logo negativo.png`, 176 x 54), already embedded in the Auth and subscription templates.

## Decision (owner, 2026-11-12)

Mockup ratified (`email-typography.html`):

- Titles in Young Serif 400, 26 px on a phone and 30 px on desktop, line height 1.15, tracking -0.015em. Fallback stack: `'Young Serif', Georgia, 'Times New Roman', serif`.
- Body, section labels, button label and verification code in Figtree. Body 15 px / 1.55, button 600 at 16 px, code 600 at 30 px with wide spacing (unchanged). Fallback stack: `Figtree, system-ui, -apple-system, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
- No display face on buttons.
- The same typographic scale in the five emails; the waitlist keeps its plain layout.

Out of scope, noticed while reading: the orange button with white text has low contrast (around 2.6:1 by my estimate, not measured with a tool), and the waitlist email uses a green and radii that are not the brand's. Both are for separate tickets.

Not verifiable from the repo: Apple Mail and Gmail, light and dark. The owner checks those with real emails.
