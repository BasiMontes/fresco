# Design brief: Fresco / slot-mark-controls

Tool session: none (Mode C, screens generated by the AI through the loaded design set)

## Mission

Design the mark controls of a calendar slot card for FRESCO-1199: "Cocinado" and "Descartar" (and, once marked, "Quitar marca" and the switch to the other mark). Today they are two full-width bordered buttons stacked on the card, taller than the recipe's own name and tags, repeated up to 21 times a week. We describe WHAT the card must accomplish; the AI composes it inside the frozen contract below. Ticket: FRESCO-1199, epic FRESCO-869.

## Method and skills loaded (provenance)

Mode C of `design-system/references/screen-design-mapping.md`. Loaded in this session before the first line of HTML: `frontend-design`, `ui-ux-pro-max`, `impeccable`, `emil-design-eng`. `DESIGN.md` wins over any generic rule of those skills (Critical Rule 14: the fidelity reference is the live UI plus `DESIGN.md` tokens). `DESIGN.md` carries no dark palette, so the mockup is light only; dark is checked at build time against the live theme tokens in `app/globals.css`.

## Design read

Operate surface (the weekly calendar). The recipe is what matters on the card; marking is a secondary, frequent action. Calm editorial language, low motion.

## Constraints from the code (read, not assumed)

- Anatomy today (`components/calendar/slot-cell.tsx`, `slot-content.tsx`): raised card with hairline border, radius card, photo area (drag handle overlay except breakfast), body with kicker (category, uppercase 12 px), title (15 px, 600), optional diet tag, then the controls. A discarded slot reads at 60 % opacity with the title struck through.
- Three states with controls: pending (`SlotMarkControls`: Cocinado, Descartar), marked inside the current week (`SlotEstadoBadge` plus `SlotChangeMarkControls`: Quitar marca, Cambiar a cocinado/descartado), marked in a past week (badge only).
- Each interactive element must be at least 44 px: the e2e step `todos los elementos interactivos miden al menos 44 px` (`expectTouchTargetsAtLeast`) measures the element's own box, so a visually small button with a larger invisible hit area would FAIL it. The saving has to come from layout, not from shrinking the box below 44 px.
- Accessible names of the mark buttons (FRESCO-1013): they start with the visible word ("Cocinado", "Descartar") and name the dish, the day and the slot. They stay.
- Narrow day column: about 190 px at 1440 px (content about 166 px), card about 358 px on a phone.
- Out of scope, owner direction (2026-11-12): when a slot can be marked does not change; only its presentation.

## Frozen design contract (inlined from DESIGN.md v2)

- Colors: primary `#0F4E0E`, tertiary `#6F5F43`, background `#FAF3E3`, surface `#F1E3C6`, surface-raised `#FBF6EC`, text `#201E1D`, border text at 16 %, error `#B03D2B`, neutral-600 `#847456` for control boundaries (FRESCO-448), accent-100 `#E1E8E0`, accent-700 `#052D05`.
- Typography: Figtree for everything on the card; caption 12 px is the minimum text size; Young Serif only on h1 and h2.
- Radius: buttons and tags full pill, card 20 px. One orange `button-action` per screen, not used here.
- Motion: calm, 150 to 250 ms, no new overshoot, reduced motion honoured.

## What the card must accomplish (functional intent)

1. The recipe (photo, kicker, name, tag) is clearly the most visible thing on the card.
2. The person can mark a dish as cooked or discard it with a thumb, in one tap, and the actions are still obvious.
3. After marking, the state is readable at a glance and, inside the current week, the mark can be taken back or switched.
4. In a past week the card shows the state and nothing to press.

## Proposals to compare (one file, same four states each)

- **Hoy**: the current look, for reference.
- **A, segmented bar**: one pill split in two, one row.
- **B, light actions**: borderless actions in one row under a hairline.
- **C, over the photo**: two icon buttons on the photo, no height in the body; the state is a chip on the photo.

States per proposal: pending, cooked in the current week, discarded in the current week, cooked in a past week. Plus a phone-width pending card.

## Hard constraints

- File name is the screen slug: `slot-mark-controls.html`.
- No token outside the frozen contract. UI copy in Spanish from Spain, no em dashes.
- Every interactive element at least 44 px in both dimensions, visible keyboard focus, text never below 12 px.
- Visible words kept where the accessible name depends on them (Cocinado, Descartar, Quitar, Cambiar).
- No new claims: dish names, photos and tags in the mockup are placeholders.

## Decision (owner, 2026-11-12)

Option C ratified: the mark controls move onto the photo as two icon buttons, with a chip for the marked state. Two adjustments asked by the owner during review: smaller icons (the visible circle is 34 px with a 13 px glyph; the button box stays 44 x 44) and a hover that opens the circle into a pill with the word. The mockup keeps all the options as a record. Options A and B save nothing at phone width (the current card already fits both buttons in one row there) and were not chosen. Option C2 (16:9 photo) would change the photo crop unified in FRESCO-447 and is left out of this ticket.

Measured on the mockup with the real 4:3 photo, pending card: 359 px today, 251 px with C in a 190 px column; 413 px today, 357 px with C at phone width.
