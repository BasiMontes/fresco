## §1. Where This App Actually Is

Read `business-data-map.md` and `business-feature-map.md` first. Note: the data-map was refreshed on 2026-10-05 (FRESCO-814) and now describes the supermarket tables. The supermarket facts below were first written from the ADRs and the design doc. The short version:

**The product core is unchanged and shipped.** Onboarding, deterministic menu generation (`ADR-0005`, no external AI), the food-safety guarantee, the editable calendar, the shopping list, the cooked/discarded learning loop (`ADR-0001`, `ADR-0006`), guest mode, progressive signup, the recipe library, own recipes (edit/delete shipped under FRESCO-236), per-user `scoreRecipe()` (`ADR-0008`), safe ingredient substitution (EPIC-FRESCO-714), the Centro de Avisos (EPIC-FRESCO-223), weekly Web Push (`ADR-0011`, `ADR-0012`), Sentry + PostHog (`ADR-0009`, `ADR-0013`), and the editorial redesign (EPIC-FRESCO-436) are all `Finalizada`. Production exists and tracks `main`; staging, dev and production deliberately share one Supabase project until real launch (`ADR-0020`, FRESCO-328).

**Monetization is live, with 0 Pro subscribers.** Stripe hosted Checkout, webhook as sole writer of subscription state, Billing Portal, dunning, daily reconciliation (`ADR-0007`, `ADR-0015`). The trial now ends explicitly (`end_behavior: cancel`, FRESCO-813). Still open on this line: the annual plan and its recurring price (FRESCO-844) and Fresco branding on the hosted Checkout page (FRESCO-845).

**The supermarket line is now in scope (ADR-0027, 2026-09-10).** "Supermarket integrations" and "price comparison" left the Out-of-Scope Blacklist. The bound is the founder's standing **no-extra-spend** rule: structured list export, affiliate deep links (Awin), and the ingredient-to-product mapping layer (FRESCO-488, shipped) are in; any paid recipe-commerce provider (Northfork / Whisk) is not, and needs a separate explicit spend decision. The moat (`ADR-0001`) is unchanged: grocery is an added-value layer, never the Free/Pro boundary, and must not pull effort off the learning loop. ADR-0027 itself states these stories stay behind concierge validation in priority order: unblocked, not prioritized.

**A source-agnostic price layer exists (ADR-0036).** Every chain is normalized into one `ProductoSupermercado` contract whose invariant is `precioEnvase` = price of the whole pack. Each connector declares `permiso` (`concedido`, `riesgo-aceptado`, `pendiente`, `rechazado`) plus a `permisoRef`, and the registry runs only the first two, fail-closed. Prices live in Postgres (chain, product, per-zone current price, history, ingredient-product matches, postcode-to-zone), readable by `authenticated`, written only by `service_role`, read through a `SECURITY INVOKER` RPC (`ADR-0032`). The four migration steps are done: connectors wrap the Mercadona and Consum catalogs (FRESCO-767), normalized prices reach the shopping list and menu cost (FRESCO-768), the per-chain `precioMercadona` / `precioConsum` fields are gone (FRESCO-769), the schema is applied and the refresh moved to a runner (FRESCO-770), and the Mercadona live connector shipped (FRESCO-771). Direct table reads respect the permission gate (FRESCO-798).

## §3. What To Implement First And Why

### Real legal sign-off (Master Sprint 0)

**Why it matters:** the plan used to read "legal banner still up." That specific banner is gone from the UI, which makes the state easier to misread: the underlying fact is that no lawyer has reviewed the Terms, Privacy or consent copy. FRESCO-434 closed on a founder decision (recorded as "not a clean closure"), and the founder chose not to reopen it until the lawyer arrives. The consent registry (`ADR-0040`) and the version stamp `provisional-2026-10-04` mean the mechanism is ready to receive final text.

**What it unblocks:** charging real users, and every Master Sprint 2 activity.

**Dependencies:** a lawyer. Not engineering.

**How an experienced engineer would scope it:**
- Smallest credible slice: when the lawyer's report or email arrives, reopen FRESCO-434 with that attachment as the exit criterion, edit the literals in `lib/legal/consent.ts`, and bump `LEGAL_TEXTS_VERSION` so users are asked again.
- Likely scope creep: building more legal UI while waiting. The mechanism is done; stop there.
- Boundary: this plan does not decide whether a chain's terms allow the data use; that remains a founder decision per chain (`ADR-0036`).

### Supermarket-layer hardening (Master Sprint 0)

**Why it matters:** the layer is not a prototype; it is the source of the prices users see. Four open stories close the gap between "works" and "safe to extend." FRESCO-808 makes the layer genuinely chain-agnostic and makes the permission gate impossible to bypass. FRESCO-846 wires the shared matcher (`emparejarIngrediente`) into the price runner; FRESCO-811 already recorded it as present but not wired. FRESCO-801 gives the runner proper secrets, an Actions dry-run and a test. FRESCO-790 proves the kill-switch works and adds an "indicative prices" clause, which also feeds the founder's legal read.

**What it unblocks:** adding Dia, Alcampo or Carrefour without redesign; the comparator (FRESCO-346); a credible answer to "what happens if Consum or Mercadona objects" (`ADR-0037` revocation is one line in `registry.ts` plus dropping the generated file).

**Dependencies:** `ADR-0036`'s contract, the applied schema (FRESCO-770), and the permission gate already enforced on reads (FRESCO-798).

**How an experienced engineer would scope it:**
- Smallest credible slice: 846 and 790 first (user-visible correctness and the kill-switch), then 808, then 801.
- Likely scope creep: a new chain or a new schema column slipping in under "agnostic." The contract can only express what every chain provides; a chain-specific extra is a contract change and needs its own decision.
- Boundary: no new calls to a real chain without a registered, permitted connector (`ADR-0036` invariant). Any new code calling Consum's endpoints needs the `ADR-0037` risk read again and small batches.

### Close the non-hygiene audit-6 tail (Master Sprint 0)

**Why it matters:** audit 6 scored the product 3,2/5 with two BLOCKERs; the blockers are handled, but the remaining stories are what move it above 4,0. The non-hygiene ones are FRESCO-837 (direct commits with a test identity, close the origin), FRESCO-810 and FRESCO-809 (architecture: remove direct DB access from UI and routes, split oversized components), FRESCO-803 (health endpoint, uptime monitor, Sentry release), FRESCO-800 (seed drift red for three weeks), and FRESCO-795/796/797 (real coverage, order-independent tests, flakiness budget). The per-area `Higiene de hallazgos BAJO` stories (FRESCO-816 to 821) are the lowest priority.

**What it unblocks:** audit sign-off and a clean baseline before any cohort traffic.

**Dependencies:** none beyond the stories themselves.

**How an experienced engineer would scope it:** work them in the audit's own severity order; keep each to the specific finding. FRESCO-815 (sanitize `Rechazos`, FRESCO-31 in `WIP`, 168 stories without an epic) is backlog hygiene and should not grow into a re-plan.

### Price comparison and the Consum live connector (Master Sprint 1)

**Why it matters:** this is the feature `ADR-0027` reversed the blacklist for. Without a comparator, the supermarket layer is a price display; with one, it is a differentiator, but only at zero cost and only on chains we are allowed to read.

**What it unblocks:** the "value-add" claim; evidence for whether a provider deal (Northfork / Whisk) is ever worth a spend decision.

**Dependencies:** FRESCO-772 is `Blocked` on the Consum consent (deadline about 2026-10-06 per `ADR-0037`; silence is not consent) and the `ADR-0037` risk read for any new Consum calls. FRESCO-346 is `Blocked` on at least two chains with usable, permitted data.

**How an experienced engineer would scope it:**
- Smallest credible slice: compare Mercadona and Consum from the already-registered connectors, using whole-pack prices only; present as indicative.
- Likely scope creep: postcode-to-zone UX. Postcode capture is a product and privacy decision not taken in `ADR-0036`; do not smuggle it in.
- Boundary: no cart push, no checkout, no resale. The deep-link spike (FRESCO-345, shipped) showed there is no consumer cart API in Spain.

### Pro annual plan and Checkout identity (Master Sprint 1)

**Why it matters:** the offer a first cohort sees is the Stripe page. FRESCO-844 is explicitly "implement and fix the recurring price in Stripe first": a wrong recurring price on a live plan is the kind of defect that is expensive after the first subscriber.

**What it unblocks:** a credible paid offer. **Dependencies:** `ADR-0007` invariants (webhook is the sole writer; price re-verified on activation and renewal), and the reconciliation job (`ADR-0015`) must learn any new price ID.

**How an experienced engineer would scope it:** change the recurring price in Stripe before touching code; extend the price-verification allowlist rather than loosening it; do not alter `prevent_client_subscription_writes()`.

### Recipe photo backfill (Master Sprint 1)

**Why it matters:** completeness of the catalog the first cohort sees. FRESCO-31 is still `WIP`. A finding from the last batches matters more than the count: the fetch query is deterministic, so retries re-pick the same bad photo; a script fix is needed before another attempt, not just quota time.

**How an experienced engineer would scope it:** fix the retry selection first, then resume batches; accept the existing placeholder degradation rather than lowering the match bar.

## §5. Features / State Machines That Block ≥2 Other Features

- **The `ProductoSupermercado` contract and the connector registry are the new load-bearing seam.** Every price shown, the menu cost estimate, the comparator, any affiliate link and any future chain depend on `precioEnvase` meaning "whole pack." A connector that violates it silently corrupts every consumer. The registry's fail-closed `permiso` gate is the only thing that keeps an unpermitted chain from running.
- **`user_profiles` remains the most contended row.** Onboarding output, subscription state written only by the Stripe webhook and the reconciliation job, and Centro de Avisos flags. Any migration must preserve `prevent_client_subscription_writes()`. The annual plan (FRESCO-844) adds a second valid price to a check that currently verifies one.
- **`recipes` now has five writers** (generation reads it; `recipe_learning_trigger`; founder batch seeding; photo backfill via `service_role`; `delete-catalog-recipe`). The supermarket layer adds a sixth reader-side coupling through ingredient-product matches, which key off the canonical ingredient vocabulary.
- **`auth.users` / anonymous identity** touches guest conversion, guest-data reassignment, account deletion, guest cleanup, and now the captcha gate (FRESCO-799) and consent registry rows (`ADR-0040`).
- **`meal_plan_recipes.estado`** is still the most load-bearing state machine (calendar, learning loop, `scoreRecipe()`); audit 6's BLOCKER A6-S1 was a reopening of its write bypass, so any new RPC touching it needs the ownership and slot checks.

## §6. Hidden Dependencies

**The permission gate and the price tables must agree.** The registry decides at runtime which connectors run; the tables hold whatever a runner wrote. FRESCO-798 made direct reads respect the gate, but the guarantee is only as strong as the single path that writes (`service_role` from the runner, FRESCO-770). If a revoked chain's rows stay in the table, a read can still serve them. `ADR-0037` revocation therefore means three things at once: flip `permiso`, stop the refresh, and purge or hide the rows. FRESCO-790's kill-switch test is the check that this actually works.

**Two chains, two source shapes, one contract.** Mercadona arrives from a community dataset (no calls to the chain) and Consum from a generated file built by calling Consum's storefront API directly; their legal exposure and their failure modes differ even though both are `riesgo-aceptado`. The contract hides the difference from consumers, but not from operations: a Consum block makes prices go stale or disappear with no fallback source (`ADR-0037`).

**The matcher is shared but not yet wired.** `emparejarIngrediente` carries one heuristic (unit family, plausible pack, whole-word term, canonical before synonyms, deterministic ties) for all chains. Until FRESCO-846 lands, the runner does not use it, so what the code claims and what the data does can diverge; FRESCO-785 (wrong-product links, diet-ignoring prices) is the class of defect this prevents.

**The Stripe webhook and the reconciliation job are two writers to the same columns.** Unchanged and still safe only while the job reconciles toward Stripe idempotently; a second price ID (annual plan) has to land in both paths together.

**Consent registry, analytics and the success bar.** `ADR-0040` makes consent append-only; the MVP success-bar query (FRESCO-793) and server-side billing events (FRESCO-813) rely on a contractual basis that is declared in Privacy text that is still provisional. Final lawyer copy can change which events are lawful, so the measurement design is coupled to the legal item.

**Centro de Avisos reads Flow 7 and the food-safety RPC.** Unchanged cross-domain coupling between Subscription and Food-Safety.

**Motion and reduced-motion remain a system-wide invariant** (FRESCO-249).

## §9. Edge Implementation Cases

- **Comparator with only one permitted chain.** If Consum is revoked or never answered, the comparator has one chain and nothing to compare. Cheapest way to defer: ship the single-chain price display as it is and hold FRESCO-346. Cheapest way to lock down: decide, under `ADR-0037`'s revocation clause, what the UI does when a chain drops out (hide, or show "no data").
- **Zones and postcodes.** The schema carries a postcode-to-zone model, but postcode capture is a product and privacy decision `ADR-0036` explicitly did not take. Defer by pricing at a default zone; lock down only after a privacy decision.
- **Affiliate links versus neutral price display.** An affiliate deep link changes the incentive of a price comparison. Decide, before FRESCO-753 turns into a build, whether links are ordered by price or by affiliate eligibility; the plan assumes by price.
- **Legal copy changes that re-ask consent.** Bumping `LEGAL_TEXTS_VERSION` makes users consent again; define what happens to a user who does not, before the lawyer's text lands.
- **Annual plan versus the verified-price check.** The webhook and reconciliation job verify one Pro price; supporting two requires extending both together, never loosening either.
- **Free/Pro is a business-logic branch, not an auth tier** (unchanged). Supermarket features, if gated at all, must reuse the server-side `plan` check; `ADR-0027` states grocery is never the Free/Pro boundary.
- **Stale supermarket data.** Prices have freshness only as good as the runner (FRESCO-770, 801). Decide the maximum age before a price is hidden or marked indicative (ties to FRESCO-790).
